Good to know

Before you ask.

Need something else?
We’re here to help.

Does this guarantee a hotel upgrade?

No. Upgrades and perks are always at the hotel’s discretion and depend on availability and policy. You’re paying for a personalised request and practical timing guidance.

Will you contact the hotel for me?

No. We prepare the request; you review and send it. You stay in control of the conversation.

Does this work for any hotel?

You can prepare a request for a boutique hotel or a large chain. Each property has its own policies, and availability varies by stay.

What happens after I pay?

Your request is generated and saved to your order. You can view it online, and we’ll email a recovery link. Your saved request is available for 30 days after purchase.

Is my booking information safe?

We save your order details and result for 30 days so you can recover them. Stripe processes payments, Google Gemini generates the wording, and SendGrid delivers email. We do not receive your card number or contact hotels on your behalf. See our privacy policy for details.
