Hotel loyalty upgrades: what your tier may include

By Raphael L. · Reviewed 10 September 2026

Loyalty status can include an upgrade, late checkout or another stay benefit, but the exact programme wording matters. A tier name alone is not enough.

Check four things

  1. Programme and tier: use the full current name.
  2. Hotel participation: a brand or property may be excluded or participate differently.
  3. Booking eligibility: rate and channel conditions can affect benefits.
  4. Benefit conditions: availability, room categories, times and guest limits may apply.

Entitlement and request are different

If a published benefit applies, ask the hotel to confirm it for the booking. If it does not apply or is conditional, you can still make a discretionary request. Do not present a possible benefit as confirmed.

Programme-aware example

I’m a [Programme] [Tier] member and understand that [benefit] may be available under the programme conditions. Could you confirm whether this hotel and booking are eligible?
