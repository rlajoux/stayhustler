Hotel request guide

Hotel upgrade email template and examples

By Raphael L. · Reviewed 10 September 2026

Use these examples freely. Replace every bracketed field, check the booking facts and ask the hotel to confirm any additional charge before changing the reservation.

Complimentary-upgrade enquiry

Subject: Room upgrade enquiry for [Check-in Date]

Hello, Reservation: [Confirmation Number] I’m looking forward to my stay at [Hotel Name] from [Check-in Date] to [Check-out Date]. I’ve booked a [Room Type]. If a higher room category is available for the full stay, would you be able to consider a complimentary upgrade? I understand this depends on availability and the hotel’s policy. Thank you, [Your Name]

Open to a paid option

Subject: Room options for [Check-in Date]

Hello, Reservation: [Confirmation Number] Could you let me know whether a higher room category is available for my stay from [Check-in Date] to [Check-out Date]? Please send the category and total additional price before making any change to my booking. Thank you, [Your Name]

How to customise the template

  • Use the hotel and room names exactly as they appear on the confirmation.
  • Say whether you are asking only about a complimentary change or are open to hearing a price.
  • Mention a programme tier only when it applies to this booking and property.
  • Keep one main request. Extra background rarely makes the booking easier to understand.

Before sending

Read the email as a request, not a demand. Remove anything untrue, add the reservation number and use a contact method associated with the hotel or booking. The hotel decides what it can offer.
