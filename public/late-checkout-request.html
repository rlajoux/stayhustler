Your email

Subject: Late checkout enquiry for 16 October

Hello,

Reservation: [Confirmation Number]

I’m looking forward to my stay at [Hotel Name] from 13 to 16 October. Would a later checkout be possible on 16 October?

Please let me know what departure time you could offer and whether there would be an additional charge before changing the booking. If the room is needed, could you let me know whether luggage storage is available instead?

Thank you,
[Your Name]

When to ask

  1. Ask whether the hotel considers late-checkout requests before arrival.
  2. Check when reception can confirm a departure time for your booking.
  3. If late checkout is unavailable, ask about luggage storage and any conditions.

What to say at reception

Would a later checkout be possible today? Please let me know what time you could offer and whether there would be a charge.
