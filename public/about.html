Hotel requests, made personal.

A clearer way to ask your hotel

StayHustler helps travellers prepare requests for hotels they’ve already booked. Choose what you’d like to ask for, add your booking details and receive an email, timing advice and a short reception script.

Raphael L.

Founder

I built StayHustler to make hotel requests easier to prepare. The aim is straightforward: useful wording, clear expectations and a traveller who feels ready to ask.

How our guidance is prepared

Your booking details and preferences supply the context. AI prepares an email, three qualified timing tips and a short reception script. You check the details, add your name and reservation number, and send the request yourself.

For supported loyalty programmes, our guide uses published programme rules with source links and a checked date. You select your programme and tier; we do not connect to membership accounts or verify your status. Your hotel confirms whether a benefit applies to your booking.

What to expect

The $7 USD payment covers one request package for one stay. It does not buy a hotel benefit. The hotel decides availability and any charge, which you should confirm before agreeing to a change.

Your saved package is available for 30 days after purchase. Keep your emailed copy for a later trip.
