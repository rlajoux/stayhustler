How This Assessment Works

This tool evaluates your upgrade probability based on factors that hotel revenue managers and front office teams actually consider: booking channel, loyalty status, demand forecasting, length of stay, and room category.

It does not predict outcomes. Hotels make upgrade decisions based on real-time inventory, and no tool can forecast that. What it does is assess how many factors are working in your favour, so you can adjust what's within your control.

The methodology is based on common revenue management practices across major hotel chains and independent properties. For a detailed explanation, see our guide on how hotel inventory systems work.
