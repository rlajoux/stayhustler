Hotel request guide

What a StayHustler hotel request includes

By Raphael L. · Reviewed 10 September 2026

StayHustler prepares a personalised email, three timing tips and a short reception script for one hotel stay. The package costs $7 USD as a single payment.

Before payment

You add the hotel, dates, room, booking channel and relevant preferences. You can choose a supported loyalty programme and tier. The review page shows those details and the exact package, but it does not reveal your personalised wording.

After verified payment

  • Your email: wording based on the supplied booking and main request.
  • Three timing tips: qualified next steps for raising and confirming the request.
  • Your reception script: a short way to ask in person.
  • Saved access: an emailed recovery link available for 30 days after purchase.

What you do

Review the package, add your name and reservation number, and send the message using the hotel’s details or the booking service. StayHustler does not contact the hotel or access its reservation system.

What the hotel decides

The hotel confirms availability, benefit eligibility and any additional charge. The $7 pays for the request package, not a hotel benefit. There is no guaranteed upgrade or late checkout.

Use the free material if it is enough

Our guides contain generic examples you can adapt yourself. The paid package is for travellers who want their booking facts, wording, timing advice and reception script brought together in one place.
