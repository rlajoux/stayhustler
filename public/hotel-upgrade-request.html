When to ask

  1. Ask whether the hotel can consider your request before arrival.
  2. If it cannot confirm in advance, ask when to check again.
  3. You can also raise the request at reception during check-in.

What to say at reception

Hello, I wanted to check whether an upgrade might be available for my stay. Could you let me know about any additional charge before changing my booking?
