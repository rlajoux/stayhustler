# Hotel loyalty research: Marriott, Hilton, IHG and Hyatt

Checked 10 September 2026 against official public pages. Research for programme and tier selection only; no account connection, membership number or product changes.

Times below are local hotel time. “Unconfirmed” means the checked sources do not establish that benefit as a tier entitlement; it does not mean the hotel cannot offer it. Availability, guaranteed entitlements, welcome choices and separately held awards must remain distinct. These are programme summaries, not a complete property eligibility database.

## Marriott Bonvoy

| Public tier | Upgrade | Checkout / arrival | Breakfast / lounge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Member | Unconfirmed | Unconfirmed | Unconfirmed |
| Silver Elite | Unconfirmed | Priority late checkout, availability dependent; no fixed time published | Unconfirmed |
| Gold Elite | Enhanced room, availability dependent; no explicit suite entitlement in the checked Gold description | Up to 14:00, availability dependent | Welcome points; breakfast/lounge unconfirmed |
| Platinum Elite | Enhanced room, including selected suites, availability dependent | Up to 16:00, guaranteed with exceptions below | Brand-dependent welcome choice and lounge access |
| Titanium Elite | Platinum stay benefits | Platinum checkout conditions | Platinum breakfast/lounge conditions |
| Ambassador Elite | Titanium stay benefits | Also Your24 request, availability dependent | Titanium breakfast/lounge conditions |

Sources: [tier overview](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/member-benefits.mi), [Gold](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/member-benefits/gold.mi), [Platinum](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/member-benefits/platinum.mi), [Ambassador](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/member-benefits/ambassador.mi).

**Critical conditions.** August 2026 terms require qualifying paid rates or award stays; ordinary OTA bookings are non-qualifying. Benefits concern the member’s own room. The 16:00 guarantee becomes availability dependent at resorts, convention hotels, Design Hotels and StudioRes. Apartments by Marriott Bonvoy guarantees 14:00, with 16:00 subject to availability. Vacation Club brands, Grand Residence Club, Ritz-Carlton Reserve and The Phoenician Residences exclude late checkout; these also exclude upgrades, as does StudioRes. Ritz-Carlton Residences Waikiki has additional exclusions. MGM, all-inclusive and yacht properties have separate terms. Your24 must be requested through Ambassador Service by 15:00 two days before arrival; approval sets matching arrival/departure times. The current Platinum upgrade clause includes suites and excludes Ritz-Carlton Club-access rooms; it does not reproduce the older Titanium-only Ritz-Carlton suite wording. Do not import that historical restriction without further verification. [Programme terms, §§1.2, 2.1, 4.3](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/terms/default.mi).

**Breakfast and lounges.** Platinum/Titanium/Ambassador breakfast is not universal. Restaurant breakfast for member plus one is a welcome choice at St. Regis, Luxury Collection, W, Sheraton, Le Méridien, Westin, Tribute Portfolio, Four Points, Aloft and Protea. Selected resorts under Marriott/JW/Delta/Autograph/Renaissance also offer it. Four Points Flex and Series have restaurant-related exceptions. Ritz-Carlton, Reserve and EDITION list points instead. Courtyard, AC, Moxy and citizenM frequently use food/beverage credits. Lounge access covers participating Marriott/JW/Delta/Autograph/Renaissance hotels, Sheraton, Le Méridien, Westin and eligible Courtyards outside the US/Canada; resort and closure rules vary. Some closures/no-lounge cases offer restaurant breakfast or points. A breakfast-inclusive brand/rate is a separate reason breakfast may be provided. [Official welcome/lounges matrix](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/member-benefits/guarantee.mi).

**Separate awards and labels.** Five Nightly Upgrade Awards are an Annual Choice option after 50 qualifying elite-night credits, with another choice after 75. Holding Platinum/Titanium alone does not establish an available award balance. These are requested upgrades, not an automatic confirmed suite on every stay. [Platinum annual choice](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/member-benefits/platinum.mi), [Titanium benefits](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/member-benefits/titanium.mi).

Lifetime Silver Elite, Lifetime Gold Elite, Lifetime Platinum Elite and retained Lifetime Titanium Elite are publicly recognised labels. They retain the corresponding stay tier, rather than a new benefit rank. [Current official selector](https://travel-partner.marriott.com/japan-airlines/en-gb), [programme terms §4.2](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/terms/default.mi).

## Hilton Honors

| Public tier | Upgrade | Checkout / arrival | Breakfast / lounge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Member | Unconfirmed | Late checkout on request, availability dependent; fixed time unconfirmed | Unconfirmed |
| Silver | Unconfirmed | Member checkout conditions | Unconfirmed |
| Gold | Availability dependent, up to Executive Floor room types | Member checkout conditions | MyWay breakfast or food/beverage credit by brand/region; lounge only with qualifying room upgrade |
| Diamond | Availability dependent, up to one-bedroom suite | Member checkout conditions | MyWay benefit; Executive Lounge access where eligible |
| Diamond Reserve | Diamond upgrade scope; highest priority | Guaranteed 16:00 on eligible stays | Also eligible Premium Clubs |
| Lifetime Diamond | Diamond stay tier | Diamond checkout conditions | Diamond benefits |

Sources: [tier benefits](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/member-benefits/), [2026 tier update](https://www.hilton.com/en/p/hilton-honors/tier-updates/), [Lifetime Diamond terms](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/terms/). No fixed programme-wide early-check-in time was established by the checked sources.

**Critical conditions.** Upgrades concern one room, available for the entire stay. No complimentary-upgrade entitlement at Embassy Suites, Hilton Garden Inn, Hampton, LivSmart Studios, Tru, Spark, Homewood Suites, Home2 Suites, Hilton Grand Vacations or Motto. Certain Maldives properties, Napua Tower and Rome Cavalieri’s Imperial Floor are also excluded. Group/certain rates may exclude upgrades. Suites exclude executive suites, villas and specialty categories. Gold lounge access requires an upgrade conferring access; Diamond/Reserve can receive Executive Lounge access without that upgrade, for member plus one registered guest. Reserve Premium Club access excludes all-inclusive Enclave rooms unless booked. Ineligible rates/ordinary third-party bookings do not earn on-property benefits. [Programme terms](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/terms/).

**Meals.** At selected non-US brands, Gold/Diamond/Reserve receive continental breakfast for member plus one registered room guest. Selected US brands and Motto globally use a daily food/beverage credit; newer YOTEL participation has property-specific rules. MyWay choices matter. Executive Lounge breakfast or Premium Club access may replace the credit; all-inclusive properties do not provide that credit. It is not a universal full-breakfast entitlement. [Tier description](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/member-benefits/), [current MyWay FAQ](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/support-faq/).

**Reserve is current, not speculative.** Hilton states the changes began 1 January 2026. One Confirmable Upgrade Reward is earned on reaching Reserve; the 120-night milestone offers another or points. A reward can confirm an available room up to a one-bedroom suite for a stay of at most seven nights on eligible paid/reward bookings at participating properties. It is a separately consumed reward. [Official announcement and FAQ](https://stories.hilton.com/releases/loyalty-upgraded-hilton-honors-introduces-faster-path-to-elite-status-and-reveals-new-premium-tier-diamond-reserve).

**Partner exception.** The current Small Luxury Hotels benefit page gives Reserve availability-dependent late checkout, not the general 16:00 guarantee. Gold/Diamond/Reserve receive continental breakfast for two and an available next-room upgrade through eligible Hilton bookings. [SLH benefits](https://www.hilton.com/en-gb/help-center/reservations/small-luxury-hotels-partnership/).

## IHG One Rewards

| Public tier | Upgrade | Checkout / arrival | Breakfast / lounge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Club Member | Unconfirmed | Up to 14:00 on request, availability dependent | Unconfirmed |
| Silver Elite | Unconfirmed | Up to 14:00 on request, availability dependent | Unconfirmed as a general entitlement |
| Gold Elite | Unconfirmed | Up to 14:00 on request, availability dependent | Unconfirmed as a general entitlement |
| Platinum Elite | Availability dependent; may include suites | Early check-in on request, availability dependent, hotel sets time; late checkout up to 14:00 | Welcome choice; breakfast/lounge unconfirmed as general tier entitlements |
| Diamond Elite | Availability dependent; may include suites | Platinum timing conditions | Breakfast available as welcome choice at participating brands |

Sources: [tier matrix](https://www.ihg.com/onerewards/content/us/en/tier-benefits), [benefit terms](https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/customer-care/member-tc/2nd-page).

**Critical conditions.** Qualifying direct/approved bookings matter; IHG explicitly preserves on-property benefits for group bookings. The member must personally check in and stay. [Membership terms, §§26–28](https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/customer-care/member-tc).

Club-room upgrades do not include lounge/services. Upgrade exceptions include residences and Ruby. Diamond breakfast covers member plus one registered room guest at Regent, InterContinental, Vignette, Hualuxe, Indigo, voco, Crowne Plaza, EVEN, Holiday Inn, Kimpton and selected Garner properties. It excludes room service/specialty restaurants and specified residences/Alliance Resorts Macau. Iberostar and Royal Resorts provide credit/points choices; Ruby provides points. Digital check-in may only offer points. Silver/Gold also have welcome choices in Greater China. Early check-in has no fixed programme time; 14:00 checkout remains availability dependent for every core tier. [Benefit conditions](https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/customer-care/member-tc/2nd-page).

**Separate milestone rewards.** Confirmable Suite Upgrades can be chosen after 20, 40 or 70 nights; Annual Lounge Membership after 40 or 70. Neither can be inferred from core status alone. A suite award covers one room for at most five nights, confirmed 14 days to 24 hours before arrival if available; a premium room can substitute. It does not add Club access. Lounge membership covers member plus one during eligible stays at participating lounges. Rate, property and certificate-type restrictions apply. [Official FAQ](https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/customer-care/frequently-asked-questions). The current terms distinguish Reward Nights from Free Night certificates and exclude already-deposited prepaid rates for suite awards. [Award terms](https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/customer-care/member-tc/2nd-page).

**Additional brand-specific statuses.** InterContinental Ambassador, invitation-only Royal Ambassador and Kimpton Inner Circle are separate recognition programmes. They should not silently replace the five core tiers. Ambassador includes Platinum status and brand-specific benefits; inspect these separately if the selector is intended to capture them. [Ambassador overview](https://www.ihg.com/intercontinental/content/us/en/loyalty), [Ambassador/Royal Ambassador terms](https://www.ihg.com/intercontinental/content/us/en/ambassador/terms-conditions.html).

## World of Hyatt

| Public tier | Upgrade | Checkout / arrival | Breakfast / lounge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Member | Unconfirmed | Unconfirmed | Unconfirmed as tier entitlement |
| Discoverist | Preferred room within booked category, availability dependent | 14:00 on request, exceptions below | Unconfirmed as tier entitlement |
| Explorist | Available upgrade excluding suites and Club-access rooms | 14:00 on request, exceptions below | Unconfirmed as automatic tier entitlement |
| Globalist | Available upgrade including standard suites | 16:00 on request, exceptions below; priority room access | Club access or breakfast at participating properties |
| Lifetime Globalist | Globalist stay benefits | Globalist timing conditions | Globalist meal/lounge benefits |

Sources: [tier chart](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/tiers-and-benefits.html), [upgrade definition](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/my-account/badges/room-upgrade.html), [checkout definition](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/my-account/badges/late-checkout.html).

**Critical conditions.** Hyatt says in-hotel benefits require an eligible paid rate or eligible award night. [Eligibility and tiers](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/tiers-and-benefits.html). The Discoverist preferred-room description excludes Hyatt Place, Hyatt House, Destination Residences and Hyatt Vacation Club. Its room selection remains within the booked category. [Upgrade badge](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/my-account/badges/room-upgrade.html). Late checkout is requested on departure day and depends on availability at casinos, resorts and Destination Residences; the badge excludes Hyatt Residence Club resorts. [Checkout badge](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/my-account/badges/late-checkout.html). Globalist lounge breakfast is continental; without a Club lounge, participating properties provide full breakfast for registered guests, capped at two adults and two children. [Breakfast badge](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/my-account/badges/club-access-or-breakfast.html).

**Separate awards.** Milestone Club Access, Suite Upgrade and Guest of Honor awards require separate eligibility/possession. Suite awards confirm standard suites for up to seven nights. Lifetime Globalist adds annual four suite awards, five Guest of Honor awards, one Category 1–7 free-night award and concierge access. [Milestones and Lifetime Globalist](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/tiers-and-benefits.html).

**Unresolved detailed exclusions.** Hyatt’s current main [programme terms](https://www.hyatt.com/help/en-US/terms/world-of-hyatt) returned no readable text in this check. The tier names, upgrade scope, checkout times and headline breakfast benefit above are verified against official pages; the full current brand/partner exception list and any fixed early-arrival entitlement are not fully verified. Do not extrapolate Hyatt-wide benefits to Mr & Mrs Smith, Venetian or other partners. Homes & Hideaways explicitly excludes normal in-hotel benefits. [Homes & Hideaways terms](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/landing/homes-and-hideaways-terms.html).

## Selection implications

- Store the named programme and named tier; a shared numeric rank cannot capture these differences.
- Keep benefit output conditional on hotel/brand and booking eligibility. With programme+tier alone, describe potential benefits and conditions rather than claim a confirmed entitlement for the trip.
- Do not infer certificate balances, milestone awards, lounge memberships or brand-specific paid/invitation status from a core tier selection.
- Offer an unknown/other status path if the selection must accept labels beyond the verified public core tiers and lifetime variants.
