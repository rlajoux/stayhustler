# StayHustler: digital marketing launch proposal

Prepared 10 September 2026. Objective: increase paid purchases at a sustainable acquisition cost.

Recommendations only. No adverts, social posts, emails, partner outreach or spending have been authorised or launched. This plan complements [the website copy review](CMO_WEBSITE_COPY_REVIEW.md).

## 1. Channel priorities

Start with English-language content for travellers who have already booked and want help with a specific hotel request. This is an audience hypothesis. Choose one initial paid-search country after checking existing customer geography and keyword forecasts; do not spread a small test across multiple markets.

| Channel | Initial role | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEO | Reach people actively researching a hotel request | Highest ongoing content priority; improve existing guides and build two useful commercial landing pages. |
| Instagram | Demonstrate the product and build a recognisable, credible brand | Start with a manageable series of practical Reels and examples. Platform fit remains a hypothesis. |
| TikTok and YouTube Shorts | Test additional distribution for the same original videos | Repurpose selectively; avoid three separate production schedules. |
| Google Search Ads | Test whether existing request intent converts economically | A small controlled experiment after landing pages and measurement are ready. |
| Travel creators and advisers | Reach travellers around an actual booked trip | Test a small number of relevant partners, with trackable referrals and bounded costs. |
| Email | Help interested subscribers return when they have a booking | Useful opt-in content and clear transactional emails; no blanket discount programme. |
| Paid Meta/TikTok and broad display | Potential later acquisition | No initial allocation. Reconsider after organic creative and unit economics provide evidence. |

Organic channels still cost time. Record content production and partner-management hours instead of describing these channels as free acquisition.

## 2. The $7 constraint

The existing base price is $7 USD for one stay. A $0.50 click converting into a purchase 2% of the time produces a $25 advertising cost per purchase. These are illustrative inputs, not observed StayHustler results or a market CPC estimate.

Use these calculations before setting bids:

> Advertising cost per purchase = cost per click / click-to-purchase conversion rate
>
> Break-even cost per click = contribution per purchase before advertising × click-to-purchase conversion rate

Contribution should reflect realised revenue after discounts and refunds, applicable taxes, payment fees, generation, email and attributable support costs. Calculate it from a consistent order cohort and follow-up window. Do not treat the full $7 selling price as available advertising budget.

For illustration only, if contribution before advertising were $5 per purchase:

| Click-to-purchase rate | Break-even CPC | CPC at a $3 target acquisition cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1% | $0.05 | $0.03 |
| 2% | $0.10 | $0.06 |
| 5% | $0.25 | $0.15 |
| 10% | $0.50 | $0.30 |

The $5 contribution and $3 target are planning examples, not verified margins or an approved target. Break-even leaves no contribution for fixed costs or profit. Avoid justifying acquisition losses with assumed repeat purchases: repeat demand is not yet established.

Before launch, retrieve keyword volume and bid forecasts for the candidate market. Google describes Keyword Planner forecasts as estimates based on historical data and campaign assumptions, not guaranteed outcomes. No Keyword Planner account data was accessed for this proposal. [Keyword Planner forecasts](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en)

## 3. SEO: own the hotel-request task

Build around room upgrades, late checkout and relevant loyalty benefits. A general travel magazine would spread limited effort too widely.

### Initial content priorities

| Topic or search phrase to validate | Content action | Commercial next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hotel upgrade email template | Improve the existing guide with useful generic examples and an explanation of what to customise. | Tailor this to my stay. |
| How to ask for late checkout | Create a distinct practical guide if the current comparison article does not satisfy this question. | Prepare a late-checkout request. |
| When to ask for a hotel upgrade | Correct the existing timing guide and explain uncertainty. | Prepare an upgrade request. |
| Hotel upgrade request | Build the commercial landing page with a complete package example and $7 price. | Build my hotel request. |
| Late checkout request | Build the corresponding commercial page with a relevant example. | Prepare a late-checkout request. |
| Hotel loyalty upgrade benefits | Improve the existing loyalty guide using the maintained catalogue and primary terms. | Check programme and tier, then choose whether a paid request is useful. |

These are candidate topics, not a keyword-volume ranking. Use Search Console to see which existing pages already attract relevant queries before creating overlapping content. Keep existing URLs where possible and distinguish the educational guide from the purchase page.

Each substantial guide should contain a direct answer, a labelled generic example, specific circumstances and exceptions, clear sources, identifiable authorship and a genuinely reviewed date. Give readers enough help to solve their question. The paid offer is convenient personalisation, timing advice and a reception script.

Google's guidance prioritises useful, original, trustworthy content and cautions against mass-producing pages for search traffic. That supports improving a small number of useful resources before publishing many similar articles. [Google helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)

### A useful asset to earn references

Publish a maintained comparison of the ten supported hotel loyalty programmes: benefit, tier, important booking conditions, source and checked date. Link into the corresponding selection flow after the feature is deployed. Do not imply live membership verification or publish unqualified benefit guarantees.

Offer the resource to suitable travel newsletters or bloggers only after it is accurate and useful enough to cite. Do not buy ranking links or generate artificial forum mentions. Genuine user questions can become new guides; retain the original meaning and permission boundaries when using customer material.

### Technical and AI-search work

Verify Search Console ownership, sitemap processing, indexation, canonical URLs, internal links and mobile page performance. Keep private results and transaction pages out of search. Check the real status first; these are verification tasks, not findings that all are broken.

Use the same clear, sourced content for AI search. Google says ordinary SEO remains relevant to its generative search features and does not require special schema for them. I would not buy a separate package promising guaranteed AI mentions. [Google's AI-search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)

Assess initial progress through relevant impressions, indexed pages and qualified visits. Thirty days is not enough to promise that a new content programme will establish profitable organic acquisition.

## 4. Social media: useful demonstrations with a human voice

My initial choice would be Instagram as the main brand account, with selected original clips adapted for TikTok and YouTube Shorts. This is a practical production choice to test, not a claim that Instagram will have the lowest acquisition cost.

Start with three short original videos per week, using a phone, readable captions and real product screens with synthetic booking data. A founder presentation or an approved creator can supply the voice. Avoid a feed made entirely of unrelated hotel footage and generic AI narration.

TikTok's creative guidance recommends a clear opening, a product benefit and a next action. Use that as a structure, without assuming it guarantees reach or sales. [TikTok creative playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_SMB_Creative_Playbook_2025.pdf)

### Repeatable formats

| Format | Example opening | What the viewer sees | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A real travel problem | Your flight is at 8pm. Checkout is at 11am. Here's what you could ask. | A short, conditional late-checkout request with a question about any fee. | See the late-checkout example. |
| Product demonstration | What does a $7 hotel request package actually include? | Email, timing tips and reception script for a clearly labelled fictional booking. | Build my hotel request. |
| Loyalty explained | Before asking for an upgrade, check what your membership may already include. | One sourced programme example with its material conditions. | Read the loyalty guide. |
| Better wording | How would you ask about a room upgrade without sounding demanding? | A concise example and why each detail is relevant. | Tailor this to my stay. |
| Founder explanation | Why StayHustler does not promise you a hotel upgrade. | A direct explanation of the service and the hotel's decision. | See the complete package. |

Public examples must remain generic. Do not reveal an unpaid viewer's personalised output, customer reservation details or private hotel correspondence. Do not imply that fictional demonstrations are real successful bookings.

### Draft 25–35-second video

> Your flight leaves in the evening, but your room's checkout time is 11am. You can ask the hotel whether a later departure is possible.
>
> “Would a 3pm checkout be available on our departure day? Please let me know if there would be a charge before changing the booking.”
>
> The hotel may need to confirm closer to departure. StayHustler prepares an email, timing advice and a reception script for your booking. The package is $7. See an example through the profile link.

Adapt length to natural delivery. An exact departure-time feature should not be presented as an existing product capability unless it collects and uses that input; this script is an educational example a traveller can adapt.

### First four weeks of topics

| Week | Video 1 | Video 2 | Video 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What the $7 package contains | A late-checkout example | How to ask about an upgrade charge |
| 2 | Details to include in a hotel request | A sourced loyalty-benefit example | What happens after payment |
| 3 | Asking politely at reception | A request for a better view | Why there is no universal upgrade guarantee |
| 4 | A founder product walkthrough | A common question from actual comments | A second version of the most useful demonstration |

Programme-specific videos depend on reviewed rules; product walkthroughs must match production. If capacity is limited, make two useful videos rather than filling a calendar with weak material.

Use saves, watch-through and relevant questions to assess the content. Use tagged links, checkout progression and purchases to assess the business. A popular clip with no qualified visits is not evidence of profitable acquisition.

## 5. SEM: a small Google Search experiment

Prepare two matching landing pages first. Each should explain the selected request, show its complete example, state $7 upfront and preserve the selection when the form opens.

Begin with one market, one Search campaign and two tightly themed groups. If volume is very low, test the groups sequentially. The keywords below are hypotheses for Keyword Planner and search-term review.

| Group | Candidate keywords | Landing page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Room upgrade | hotel upgrade request; hotel upgrade email; request a room upgrade | /hotel-upgrade-request |
| Late checkout | late checkout request; late checkout email; request late checkout | /late-checkout-request |

Start narrowly with exact match, adding selected phrase matches only when useful. These searches can still be informational, so the ad should clearly qualify the paid service. Exact match can include close variants; review actual search terms rather than assuming literal matching. [Google keyword guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10039665?hl=en), [close variants](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9342105?hl=en)

Exclude unrelated searches such as hotel employment, flight upgrades and accommodation booking. Keep clearly free-only searches out of the initial paid-package test; serve them through useful organic guides. Do not blindly exclude the word “template” across everything, because it also describes a relevant customer task.

### Draft advert assets

**Room-upgrade headlines**

- Hotel Upgrade Request
- $7 Hotel Request Package
- Email, Timing & Desk Script

**Description**

> Get an email, timing advice and a reception script. $7 per stay. You send the request.

**Late-checkout headlines**

- Late Checkout Request
- Ask Your Hotel Clearly
- One Stay. No Subscription.

**Description**

> Prepare your late-checkout request for $7. Your hotel confirms availability and any fee.

These are draft assets, not uploaded adverts. Avoid “get a free upgrade”, guaranteed results and language suggesting affiliation with a hotel programme. Keep the advert, page, product and Stripe amount consistent.

Do not start with Performance Max, broad display or automatic expansion across multiple channels. For this small test, the aim is to understand which actual searches produce purchases. Reconsider broader campaign types only with stronger conversion evidence.

## 6. Partners and email

### Small relevant partners

Shortlist travel creators, independent travel advisers, honeymoon planners and newsletters whose readers have upcoming hotel stays. Assess audience relevance, credible engagement and ability to demonstrate the product, not follower count alone.

Test a handful of partners with tagged links and clear attribution. A modest commission per eligible full-price order could reduce upfront risk, but its amount must fit the actual acquisition allowance. Agree refund treatment, payout timing and paid-relationship disclosure before launching. Referral tracking and payout operations are additional work, not an existing feature to advertise.

Do not pay large sponsorship fees for unmeasured exposure. Do not contact anyone as part of this proposal. Earned coverage should come from a useful resource or demonstration, not fabricated customer stories.

### Email

Offer an optional signup for a useful generic resource, such as a hotel-request checklist. Keep it separate from access to the purchased package.

Proposed welcome content:

1. Deliver the promised resource and explain the paid package with an example.
2. In a later useful message, explain how to check relevant hotel or loyalty conditions.
3. Give subscribers a clear route back when they have a booking.

Frequency and any automated sequence need a separate implementation decision. Keep marketing preferences distinct from transactional delivery. Do not assume every buyer wants newsletters or that every form abandonment authorises a reminder.

## 7. Suggested first-month allocation

If choosing a small cash pilot, I would propose a maximum **$300 USD envelope**, excluding existing hosting and the founder's time:

| Item | Proposed maximum |
| --- | --- |
| Google Search learning test | $200 |
| Optional editing, captions or small creative expense | $100 |
| Paid social, broad display and SEO retainers | $0 |

This is a proposed learning budget, not a minimum commitment or a forecast of returns. Spend nothing on adverts until measurement, landing pages and the contribution calculation are ready. If keyword economics look implausible, retain the search budget.

Use a separately agreed limit for each search group. For example, pause a group after $30 with no recorded purchase, reconcile any delayed conversions and inspect relevance before resuming. This is a financial control, not a statistically conclusive rejection of the market. Pause immediately for broken checkout or material fulfilment problems.

Monitor actual cumulative spend rather than treating an average daily budget as a hard daily ceiling. Google allows daily spending above the average budget within its stated limits. [Google Ads spending limits](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486637?hl=en)

Do not spend the remaining allocation merely because it exists. Scale only when repeated full-price cohorts show an acceptable acquisition cost and reliable fulfilment. If paid clicks cannot fit the economics, strengthen organic and partner channels; test a higher-value offer separately before reopening a large paid campaign.

## 8. Measurement and work sequence

GA4 tags and a purchase event are present in the repository. That is not proof that production attribution is complete or that all events arrive correctly. The results page emits a purchase event from payment-backed order data, but browser visits and tags can be missed. Reconcile analytics against verified orders and Stripe before judging a campaign.

Track the journey from source to landing page, request started, review reached, checkout started, verified paid order and usable result. Deduplicate purchases by order ID. Separate full-price orders, discounts, free grants, refunds and internal tests. Check attribution across the main site, app subdomain and payment return.

Keep booking details, membership information and email addresses out of ordinary analytics event labels and URLs. Review the applicable consent configuration before adding advertising tags. Tagged campaign links should describe the channel, content and campaign, not personal data.

Report three levels of evidence:

- **Commercial:** full-price paid purchases, acquisition cost and contribution after marketing.
- **Journey:** qualified visits, form progress and checkout conversion by source and request.
- **Quality:** successful delivery, use of the package, usefulness feedback, refunds and support burden.

Search impressions and social engagement help diagnose discovery. They are not substitutes for purchases. Hotel outcomes are observational feedback and do not establish causal upgrade lift.

### Proposed sequence

| Stage | Work | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Apply the agreed website copy, validate measurement and calculate contribution. Review current search queries and country data. | Are the product and economics ready for a cash test? |
| Week 2 | Improve the strongest existing guides; prepare the two landing pages and initial videos. | Can target travellers understand and use the offer? |
| Week 3 | Run the approved small Search pilot if viable; continue social content and prepare a partner shortlist. | Which searches and demonstrations produce qualified buyers? |
| Week 4 | Review paid cohorts, search progress, content production cost and customer usefulness. | Stop, revise or continue each activity on its own evidence. |

This sequence is a proposal, not a scheduled automation. A profitable channel, keyword volumes, acquisition cost and repeat-purchase value remain unverified. The first month should establish whether the offer and distribution deserve further investment, not promise scale.
