# Loyalty research: Best Western, Radisson and GHA DISCOVERY

Official sources checked 10 September 2026. Research for programme/tier selection, not an account connection or an implemented benefit guarantee. Claims require the hotel, rate and traveller to satisfy the applicable conditions.

## Best Western Rewards

| Tier | Relevant published benefits |
| --- | --- |
| Blue | Member rates; no early/late checkout entitlement shown in the current benefit grid. |
| Gold | Water and arrival points, with location restrictions. |
| Platinum | Early check-in and late checkout, subject to availability. No fixed hour is published in the grid. |
| Diamond | Inherits Platinum's timing benefit; higher points bonus. |
| Diamond Select | Inherits the same timing benefit; higher points bonus. |

The current grid does not establish a universal tier-based room upgrade, breakfast or lounge entitlement. Display those as unverified unless another applicable official rule supports them. [Official tier grid and footnotes](https://www.bestwestern.com/en_US/best-western-rewards.html)

The terms exclude third-party reservations from elite benefits. A traveller's selected status alone is therefore insufficient to promise them. [Programme terms, Elite Status](https://www.bestwestern.com/en_US/legal/bwr-terms-conditions.html)

## Radisson Rewards

| Tier | Relevant published benefits |
| --- | --- |
| Club | Selected-hotel priority queue and participating food/beverage discounts. |
| Premium | Next-category upgrade and early/late checkout, subject to availability. |
| VIP | Best-category upgrade subject to availability; selected suites only. Breakfast for member plus one registered companion; selected VIP areas. |

Suite availability is property-specific; the benefit grid cautions against guarantees for residences, apartments and long-stay rates. [Official benefit comparison](https://www.radissonhotels.com/en-us/rewards/benefits)

The terms define early/late checkout as up to two hours before/after the hotel's published times, subject to availability. Upgrades apply to the member's room. VIP breakfast has participation and rate conditions; Asia-Pacific coverage is buffet breakfast. Selected VIP-area access should not be relabelled as universal executive-lounge access.

Scope is Europe, Middle East, Africa and Asia Pacific. Radisson-branded properties in the Americas require the appropriate current programme check. Online-travel-company rates are explicitly described as eligible for benefits even where they do not earn points, so do not apply a universal OTA exclusion. [Official programme terms, definitions and member benefits](https://www.radissonhotels.com/en-us/terms-and-conditions)

## GHA DISCOVERY

| Tier | Relevant published benefits |
| --- | --- |
| Silver | Member rates, D$ earning and Wi-Fi; no standard upgrade or checkout benefit listed. |
| Gold | Higher D$ earning; no standard upgrade or checkout benefit listed. |
| Platinum | One-category upgrade and checkout until 15:00, subject to availability; welcome amenity. |
| Titanium | Two-category upgrade, check-in from 11:00 and checkout until 16:00, subject to availability; enhanced amenity and brand benefits. |

The four public tiers are confirmed by the [official programme overview](https://www.ghadiscovery.com/gha-discovery-benefits). The Platinum/Titanium upgrade and timing differences are described in the [official tier progression guide](https://www.ghadiscovery.com/discover-brands-get-rewarded).

Titanium breakfast applies at participating brands/hotels, not the entire alliance. The participating list has effective dates, including additions in 2026. An upgrade onto an executive or club floor does not automatically include its lounge benefits. Special suites, residences and villas may be excluded from upgrades. Benefits apply to the member's room on eligible rates; third-party online retailer rates are classified as ineligible. Early arrival should be requested at least two days ahead; late checkout is requested at arrival. Check the current detailed rules before classifying an individual booking. [Terms, sections 1, 4.6, 4.7 and 4.12](https://www.ghadiscovery.com/terms-conditions)

## Recommended handling

Store programme and tier separately. Ask the traveller to confirm the hotel brand and booking channel where needed. Treat source omissions as unknown, not a confirmed refusal by the hotel. Include programme status in the generated request only for a relevant participating property, and qualify every availability-dependent request. This research does not verify an individual customer's membership or booking eligibility.
