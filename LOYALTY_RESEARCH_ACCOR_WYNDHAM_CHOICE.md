# Loyalty research: ALL Accor, Wyndham Rewards and Choice Privileges

Checked: **10 September 2026**. Official public sources only. Planning evidence for programme and tier selection; no product changes. A listed entitlement can still require a request, an eligible booking and hotel availability. **Unknown** means this research did not establish a programme-wide entitlement, not that a property cannot provide it.

## ALL Accor

The public grid includes six named statuses, including invitation-only **Limitless**. The separate introductory article describes only the five generally attainable statuses. Use the full grid for the tier list. [A1] [A2]

| Tier | Relevant stay benefits |
| --- | --- |
| Classic | Member rate and premium Wi-Fi. |
| Silver | Adds welcome drink, priority reception and availability-dependent late checkout. |
| Gold | Adds room upgrade and welcome amenity; early check-in **or** late checkout. |
| Platinum | Early check-in **and** late checkout; lounge access and Asia-Pacific breakfast, with conditions below. Separately earned Suite Night Upgrades. |
| Diamond | Platinum benefits plus weekend breakfast elsewhere; dining/spa rewards. |
| Limitless | Diamond benefits; dedicated personal assistant/concierge. Invitation required. |

Sources: tier mechanics and time combinations [A1]; breakfast and general tier benefits [A2]; Limitless access [A6].

- **Timing:** Silver onward is request-based and availability-dependent. No universal clock time or guaranteed extension was established. [A1]
- **Room upgrade:** Gold onward, next category if available at check-in; otherwise an enhanced room within the booked category. Excludes villas and unique, speciality/presidential suites. Raffles stops at Junior Suite; Fairmont Gold Floor is excluded. Not offered at ibis, ibis Styles, Adagio Original/Access/Premium, JO&JOE, or hotels without different room types. [A1]
- **Lounge:** Platinum onward, member and registered room companions, subject to hotel rules. Fairmont Gold Lounge begins at **Diamond**, capped at two adults and two children; individual lounges can impose stricter child policies and opening hours. [A3]
- **Breakfast:** Diamond onward on Saturday/Sunday mornings generally; Friday/Saturday in Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan, Egypt, Algeria, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Iraq, Syria, Sudan, Libya and Comoros. Platinum onward receives daily breakfast in Asia-Pacific, except Georgia, Kazakhstan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan in the detailed terms. Covers room occupants; excludes room service/tips and non-participating hotels. Hoxton provides a breakfast bag. [A4]
- **Booking:** Benefits follow status at check-in and eligible stays, excluding day use. Eligible channels include Accor direct channels and connected traditional agencies; third-party online agencies/resellers do not qualify. [A4]
- **Certificate distinction:** Suite Night Upgrades guarantee a suite when redeemed against available qualifying inventory at booking, not merely through holding Platinum. Two are earned at 60 nights/14,000 Status points; additional awards follow points milestones, capped at twelve annually. Participating luxury/premium hotels only. [A5] New bookings only; one award covers one night; room/suite pairs and rate exclusions apply. [A4]
- **Other boundaries:** Gold room-availability guarantee needs three days’ notice; Platinum onward two. This does not guarantee a particular room category. Diamond dining/spa rewards and status gifting are separate rewards, not automatic food credits at every stay. [A1] [A4]

**Open conflict:** The grid names fewer excluded Asia-Pacific countries than the terms; the lounge directory permits restaurant or lounge breakfast, while the terms direct guests to the lounge where one exists. Use the detailed country exclusions and verify the venue with the hotel. Fairmont Gold Lounge eligibility is expressly confirmed by the current directory. [A1] [A3] [A4]

## Wyndham Rewards

| Tier | Qualification | Relevant stay benefits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue | Enrolment | Basic Wi-Fi. |
| Gold | 5 qualifying nights | Preferred room choice; late checkout. |
| Platinum | 15 qualifying nights | Gold benefits plus early check-in. |
| Diamond | 40 qualifying nights | Platinum benefits plus suite upgrade and selected-brand welcome amenity. |
| Titanium | Invitation/designation | Diamond stay benefits; separate annual points bonus and optional Avis enrolment. |

Sources: public benefit grid [W1]; Titanium terms effective 1 February 2026 [W3].

- **Timing:** Gold onward: request up to **two hours after** normal checkout. Platinum onward: request up to **two hours before** normal check-in. Both depend on hotel availability; no universal 14:00/16:00 guarantee. [W2]
- **Room choice:** Gold/Platinum preferred-room requests remain within the category booked. Diamond suite upgrades are decided at check-in, require an advance reservation and available suites, and apply only to the member’s room. Titanium inherits these stay benefits. [W2] [W3]
- **Eligible stays:** Hotel benefits apply to qualified paid stays, Free Nights and Points + Cash at participating hotels. Redemption-only destinations and other property types are excluded. [W2] Third-party online agency reservations are excluded from qualified stays. [W4]
- **Welcome amenity:** Diamond/Titanium, one snack/beverage per stay, only at the brands listed in the terms: AmericInn, Baymont, Dazzler, Dolce, Esplendor, Hawthorn, La Quinta, Microtel, Ramada, TRYP, Vienna House, WaterWalk, Wingate, Wyndham Alltra, Wyndham Grand, Wyndham and Wyndham Garden. [W2] [W3]
- **Titanium:** Not a nights-based public qualification tier. Eligible parties include hotel principals, nominated friends and sponsor-designated individuals. It inherits Blue through Diamond benefits **except accelerated earnings**; its conditional annual bonus is 30,000 points. This is not a suite certificate. [W3]

**Open points:** Programme-wide status breakfast and hotel-lounge access were not established by the current grid or terms. Mark unknown rather than promising them or asserting every hotel excludes them. The grid’s brief suite-upgrade wording must be read with the availability conditions. [W1] [W2]

## Choice Privileges

The refreshed programme began **1 January 2026**. Titanium is current; Your Extras was replaced by Milestone Rewards. [C3]

| Tier | Annual qualifying nights or credits | Relevant stay benefits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member | Enrolment | Member rates; other stay entitlements not established here. |
| Gold | 5 nights or 10,000 credits | Elite early/late requests, selected-brand upgrades, welcome gift and participating-property parking. |
| Platinum | 15 nights or 30,000 credits | Same researched stay-benefit categories as Gold. |
| Diamond | 35 nights or 70,000 credits | Adds selected Radisson-brand breakfast and conditional room-availability guarantee. |
| Titanium | 55 nights or 110,000 credits | Diamond stay benefits plus annual Titanium Travel Award. |

Sources: current tier thresholds [C1] [C4]; all four elite tiers qualify for the stated request benefits [C2].

- **Timing:** All elites can request check-in up to **two hours early**. Checkout is up to **14:00 local** in the US, Canada, Mexico, Central America and Caribbean; up to **two hours after normal checkout** in Europe, Middle East, Asia-Pacific and most other international locations. Availability-dependent; request checkout before the normal deadline. [C2]
- **Upgrades:** Cambria/Ascend worldwide; Radisson, Radisson Blu, Radisson RED, Radisson Collection, Radisson Individuals and Park Plaza in the Americas. Hotel decides at check-in for the member’s room; suites are possible, with no specific category guaranteed. [C1]
- **Breakfast:** Diamond/Titanium, member plus one registered guest at participating restaurants of those six Radisson brands in the Americas. Breakfast format varies; room service excluded. No worldwide all-brand entitlement established. [C1] [C2]
- **Welcome/parking:** Gift options vary by brand and participation; do not promise every member a choice between points and food. Reserved parking is availability-dependent and may still be chargeable. [C1]
- **Booking:** The welcome gift requires a points-eligible stay. Third-party agency bookings and reward nights are not points-eligible under that definition; do not assume this restriction also applies identically to every other benefit. [C2]
- **Room-availability guarantee:** Diamond/Titanium, selected upgrade brands; 48 hours from local noon, standard room at prevailing rate. Reward/Points + Cash stays, major nearby events and emergencies are excluded. [C2]
- **Award distinction:** Titanium’s annual coupon reduces points by 50% for one eligible reward reservation, one room, up to seven nights. It requires redemption at selected properties. [C3] Booking and stay must finish in its issue year; website/app only; Preferred Hotels excluded. [C2] Milestone Rewards require qualifying activity and a selection; points default after six months if unclaimed. [C1]

**Radisson boundary:** Radisson Rewards Americas was integrated into Choice Privileges in July 2023. It is not a separate current tier programme. [C5] Radisson brands outside the Americas belong to an unaffiliated company; do not apply Choice tier benefits to those properties solely from the brand name. [C6]

**Open points:** No general Choice elite hotel-lounge entitlement established. The public benefit page still calls points sharing “Coming Soon”, while the FAQ mentions an expected March 2026 launch; do not mark that feature live from the date alone. [C1] [C3]
